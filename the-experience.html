The experience

Wake with the lagoon.
Let the day unfold.

There is no itinerary here. Just water, shade, open air and enough time to enjoy all three.

A DAY AT ICHKIICHPAN

Nothing to rush.
Nowhere you have to be.

The best days here are simple: start by the water, disappear into the shade when the sun gets high, gather outside, then return to the dock as evening arrives.

01

Morning

The water
comes first.

Step straight into the lagoon, take a kayak out while the water is calm, or stay at the end of the dock and watch the color change.

DirectLagoon access

Through the day

Move with
the light.

Ichkiichpan shifts throughout the day. Water in the morning. Shade at midday. Open air again when the heat softens.

02

Between water and jungle

Find your own
corner of the property.

Some moments are shared. Others are quieter. Move from the lagoon to shaded paths, from the cabin to the palapa, or simply find somewhere to read and disappear for an hour.

When you want more

Bacalar is there.
It does not have to be the plan.

Head out when you want restaurants, cafés and another side of the lagoon. Or stay exactly where you are. Ichkiichpan works best when leaving feels optional.
